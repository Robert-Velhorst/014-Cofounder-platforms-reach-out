# Sri Lanka and Nepal discovery review

Date: 2026-10-02. Narrow official-page review, not a whole-inventory refresh or
proof of membership, delivery, security claims or worldwide completeness.

## Decisions and evidence

- **InstaLanka — publish discovery-only.** The [homepage](https://www.instalanka.com/)
  and [about page](https://www.instalanka.com/about) advertise Sri Lankan co-founder
  matching. The [matching path](https://www.instalanka.com/matching) redirected to
  login; [registration](https://www.instalanka.com/register) was readable but not
  submitted. Pricing and member access were not verified. Guessed `/terms` and
  `/privacy` paths were inaccessible through the reader: this does not prove that
  policies do not exist. Counts and sample startups were not used as operating proof.
- **Founders Nepal — publish as community, not dedicated matcher.** The
  [homepage](https://foundersnepal.com/) describes co-founder networking; the
  [FAQ](https://foundersnepal.com/faq) claims free core usage with optional Premium.
  [Terms sections 3–5](https://foundersnepal.com/terms) require adult eligibility
  and prohibit automated access and copying without permission. This is a source
  policy summary, not legal advice or a permission grant. No account or message
  was created; community publicity does not authorize importing personal data.
- **BuildGara — hold as an upcoming-module lead.** Its
  [homepage](https://buildgara.com/) labels the co-founder module upcoming. The
  [terms](https://buildgara.com/terms) describe a construction/investor marketplace,
  creating a product-description discrepancy. No working founder matching was
  established. Do not list marketing functionality as a live matching service.
- **Startup SL — hold pending reliable source retrieval.** A
  [registration page](https://www.startupsl.lk/register) was initially returned,
  but rereading timed out and the [homepage](https://www.startupsl.lk/) returned a
  gateway error. Current co-founder-specific functionality remains unverified.
- **MAARG — not a dedicated co-founder matcher on inspected evidence.** Startup
  India's [SETU page](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/startup-setu.html)
  was surfaced in search as mentor/startup matching. No direct-page deep read was
  completed in this tranche, so this remains a screening lead, not a published
  source or a definitive exclusion from future ecosystem research.

## Method and limits

Three searches targeted India government matchmaking, Sri Lankan founder matching
and Nepal founder matching. Direct reads covered the four candidate home/entry
pages, available access pages and relevant policy/FAQ paths. The research skill's
Firecrawl/Exa tools were absent; available web search/read tooling was used. Reader
failures and page-level discrepancies were retained rather than filled by inference.
Some page reads were truncated; no claim of reading every policy clause is made.
Only clauses actually visible support the recorded restrictions. Privacy pages
were retrieved but not used to certify compliance or operator practices.

English searches dominate this tranche; Sinhala, Tamil and Nepali native-language
coverage, other countries, exact groups, actual signup/contact entitlement and
operator permission remain open. No private profile, form submission, account,
payment, send, purchase or publication occurred. Changes are local repository data.

## Product boundary

Catalogue entries carry date, aliases, explicit unknowns and all integration
verification flags false. Neither new listing enters the manual-admission allowlist.
Tests cover region/alias search and rejection of intake, CSV import and outreach
source declaration for six ID/name/domain variants. No UI layout changed. This
does not replace connected browser or live platform acceptance.
