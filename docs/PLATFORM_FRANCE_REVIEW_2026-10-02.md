# French-language co-founder matching: source review

Reviewed October 2, 2026. Three independently identified dedicated discovery
routes were added locally: Cofondateur.fr, Cofoundr and CoFoundry. They are not
aliases of one another, working integrations or endorsed providers. Catalogue
source now has **67 records: 21 dedicated, 23 community/channel, 23 program**;
23 remain manual-reviewed and 44 discovery-only. Prior 64-source package receipts
are historical and do not qualify this new source increment.

## Questions, method and limits

This tranche asked: which French-language dedicated routes are missing; what
access/cost constraints are advertised; and what data/contact permissions or
operator uncertainties need to remain visible? Two search queries were used:
`StartHawk cofounder matching official pricing terms` and
`cofounder matching platform France official trouver associé plateforme`.
The preferred research connectors were unavailable; the available web tool was
used for discovery and direct first-party page reads. Snippets and testimonials
were leads, not acceptance evidence. No signup, application, purchase, contact,
member-directory traversal or provider action was performed. Incidental public
profile text was not imported or retained as candidate data.

Confidence is high in the observed public-page descriptions and catalogue
identity separation, low in actual availability/member behaviour, and unproven
for compliance, results, account access and permitted third-party workflows.
This is not an exhaustive France or Francophone inventory.

## Cofondateur.fr

The public site describes matching between project owners and entrepreneurial
talents. Success times, user totals, qualification and AI performance are the
provider's claims, not verified outcomes.
[Public site](https://www.cofondateur.fr/).

Terms restrict registration to eligible adult individuals or legal entities;
registration may be refused. Freelance/agency selling is outside the intended
use, project owners' contact details must not be passed to third parties, and
site-content reuse requires written agreement. Founder programmes commit for
three months and renew automatically afterward; instalments are not cancellable
monthly subscriptions. These constraints support discovery only, not import or
outreach approval.
[Terms](https://www.cofondateur.fr/conditions-generales-d-utilisation-et-de-vente).

The talent pricing page advertises a limited free plan, Standard EUR 29.90/month
and Premium EUR 59.90/month excluding VAT, plus quoted sourcing. Paid AI-message
and contact-visibility features are not rights for this app to collect contacts
or generate/post messages. Checkout and final tax-inclusive costs were not tested.
[Talent plans](https://www.cofondateur.fr/subscription/pricing).
The privacy page describes the provider's own processing, not permission for our
separate system to reuse personal data.
[Privacy](https://www.cofondateur.fr/politique-de-confidentialite-et-raitements-des-donnees-personnelles).

## Cofoundr

The site advertises French-language founder matching, matched-user chat, a
EUR 0/month beta and EUR 5/month supporter extras. The page also describes both
ten matching criteria and a four-criterion score; quality, feature consistency,
continued free access and the statistics were not independently established.
[Public beta page](https://cofoundr.fr/).

Terms require legally capable adults, valid email and accurate information;
matching means mutual interest inside that service, not permission for contact
from a separate app. Unauthorized commercial use is restricted, and users must
check others' information themselves.
[Terms](https://cofoundr.fr/terms).
The legal notice has unfinished company form, registration, address, phone and
publication-director fields. Operator identity remains unverified; the listing
shows that warning instead of presenting the provider as vetted.
[Legal notice](https://cofoundr.fr/legal).
Privacy/security statements are provider assertions, not an audit or data-reuse
grant. No sensitive data should be submitted without a separate operator review.
[Privacy](https://cofoundr.fr/privacy).

## CoFoundry

The student page restricts access to partner schools. Its partner section invites
a first institution rather than confirming a usable school; this conflicts with
the surrounding already-available wording. The history page advertises a
September 2026 launch. Neither a launch statement nor free-student marketing
establishes an available institutional account path.
[Student access](https://www.cofoundry.fr/etudiants),
[History](https://www.cofoundry.fr/notre-histoire).

Terms require higher-education membership, prohibit unauthorized data extraction,
and describe basic access as free only for partner-school students; premium
features may be offered, schools need quoted annual subscriptions, and investor
access is selected and paid. Adults or parental authorization are mentioned.
[Terms](https://www.cofoundry.fr/legal/cgu).
Privacy specifies an over-15 parental-authorization route for minors. That is a
provider constraint, not a change to this app's admission rules. Privacy,
contract-generation and security claims were not independently validated.
[Privacy](https://www.cofoundry.fr/legal/confidentialite).
A legal notice identifies a company, but no independent registry verification or
operational assurance was performed.
[Legal notice](https://www.cofoundry.fr/legal/mentions-legales).

The catalogue links to the readable student route. The root homepage returned
effectively no readable body through this tool; that does not prove outage or
closure. Dedicated matching is an advertised service classification, not proof
of current open enrolment or a successful pairing.

unpublished research leads here: supporting access/pricing/rights evidence was
not directly reviewed, so no operating or permission claim is made. Wider
French/Francophone services, schools, exact communities and country eligibility
remain open work; three additions cannot prove worldwide completeness.
