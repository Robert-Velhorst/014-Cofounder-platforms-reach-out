## Completion criteria for the worldwide requirement

The Australia/New Zealand/Fiji increment adds startups.org.nz and Fishburners as
communities and Fiji Innovation Hub as an indirect ecosystem route. It does not
establish dedicated matching, open Fiji intake, paid member access or coverage
across the Pacific. See the [twelfth source supplement](PLATFORM_SOURCE_REVIEW.md#twelfth-coverage-supplement-2026-10-02-australia-new-zealand-and-fiji).

Later Southeast Asia increment: QBO and Launchgarage add Philippines ecosystem
discovery, not dedicated matching. Country-specific Antler Vietnam/Indonesia
observations are recorded without adding duplicate brand records or changing
workflow admission. See the [eleventh source supplement](PLATFORM_SOURCE_REVIEW.md#eleventh-coverage-supplement-2026-10-02-philippines-and-antler-country-distinctions).
This narrows a discovery gap; current events, access, local-language workflows,
other Southeast Asian countries and permission acceptance remain unresolved.

1. Maintain a deduplicated discovery register spanning dedicated services, public
   communities, exact permission-reviewed groups, events, programs, university and
   local startup ecosystems. Separate aliases, defunct brands and unresolved leads.
2. Research each regional/country/language tranche using official local sources,
   not only English global-platform lists. Record queries, dates, primary evidence,
   account availability, pricing, access requirements and unresolved gaps.
3. Before publishing a lead, directly read supporting evidence. Search snippets,
   historical pages, member counts and marketing claims alone are insufficient.
4. Keep provenance, discoverability and workflow admission separate. Review the
   exact permitted import/manual-contact path before admitting a source. Preserve
   data rights, individualized drafts, opt-outs, approvals, rate limits and receipts.
   Paid memberships, forms and external sends require separate authorization.
5. Validate customer search/rendering and fail-closed admission tests after each
   tranche. Independently approve and perform live-provider acceptance where needed;
   mocked/unit/fixture tests cannot substitute for that acceptance.
6. Establish an operator-owned refresh/removal procedure and visible stale/closed
   warnings. A dated reviewed inventory can be maintained; an unbounded assertion
   of every platform forever cannot be proven by a finite count.
