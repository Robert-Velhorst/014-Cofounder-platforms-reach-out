## Official evidence and implications

- Communitech's [application page](https://www.communitech.ca/founders/apply/)
  limits membership to own-product technology businesses with an Ontario connection
  and expects incorporation within 12 months. Agencies, consultancies and service
  businesses are excluded. No form submitted or account admission tested.
- [Founder Connect](https://marketplace.communitech.ca/products/free-membership)
  advertises community events, peer sessions and a Slack channel at CAD 0, but the
  listing is marked sold out. That is not proof of open free entry. The separate
  [Ignite listing](https://marketplace.communitech.ca/products/startup-membership)
  shows CAD 50 and monthly/annual variants; selected-term checkout and taxes are
  unverified. No membership bought or private group joined.
- [Founder service terms](https://communitech.ca/about/policies/terms-of-service.html)
  prohibit spam/unauthorized solicitation and require rights for supplied personal
  data. [Website terms](https://www.communitech.ca/about/policies/terms-of-use.html)
  restrict content reuse without written permission. These are published operator
  terms, not a legal enforceability conclusion or API/data/contact permission.
- Startup Canada's official communities page at
  `https://www.startupcan.ca/explore/startup-communities/` lists local chapters,
  describes free community participation and mostly open events, but retains
  pandemic-era virtual-program wording. French-language chapter links failed
  retrieval; current chapter entry and language access were not established.
  Its terms at `https://www.startupcan.ca/terms-of-use/` require prior permission
  to link to the site, prohibit email harvesting/solicitation and restrict reuse.
  Accordingly no customer-facing card/link was added. Permission was not requested.
