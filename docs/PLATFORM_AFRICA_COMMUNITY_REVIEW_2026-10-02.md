## Method and gaps

Deep-research guidance separated relevance, access/pricing and permission questions.
Eight queries across those questions used the available web tool because
Firecrawl/Exa are absent. Nine primary-domain pages were directly retrieved/read;
CcHUB /coworking retrieval failed. Extracted CcHUB/iHUB pages also show human-
verification wording; text retrieval is not a successful membership/browser
access check. No protection bypass, account creation, signup, community joining,
booking, subscription, email, form submission or paid commitment occurred.

Unresolved: country-wide coverage beyond these hubs; other African countries;
Swahili and other native-language workflows; exact private-group rules, membership
availability, Nigeria-specific prices, profile collection/contact rights and
provider acceptance. These routes narrow one discovery gap, not the full goal.
