# Spanish-language and Latin America source review — October 23, 2026

## Decision

Hold WeFoundy, cofound.lat and cofound.space outside the runtime catalogue for
now. The first is a dedicated discovery candidate needing policy/access review;
the second now has readable public evidence but unresolved app/access conflicts;
the third is explicitly pre-launch. They are not aliases of one another or of
the existing cofound.biz record. No account, integration, import, outreach or
paid-service permission is established. The 68-record catalogue is unchanged.

## WeFoundy

The [Spanish homepage](https://wefoundy.com/) advertises founder/project discovery
and free profile creation, browsing and receipt of messages. Initiating a
conversation uses one credit; publishing a project uses five. Advertised packs
are EUR 9.99/10 credits and EUR 29.99/50 credits, without recurring subscriptions.
These are untested offers, not checkout, tax, eligibility or perpetual-price
guarantees; the service is not free for every matching/contact action.

Spanish-language public content and examples do not establish supported-country
or member access. Headline profile totals and zero-valued live counters conflict
in the retrieved page; neither is an accepted activity metric. Testimonials,
compatibility scores and verification labels are provider claims, not independently
proven outcomes. No profile details were copied into this repository.

The retrieved footer did not expose substantive privacy/terms links. Direct reads
of the conventional `/privacy` and `/terms` paths were inaccessible through the
reader. This is a retrieval/policy-discovery gap, not proof that no policies exist.
Resolve actual operator, adult eligibility, paid-contact limits and reuse/contact
rules before a catalogue decision. No credit purchase, signup or message occurred.

## Cofound at cofound.lat

The [homepage](https://cofound.lat/) rendered in an isolated browser after the text
reader returned no readable body. Its country FAQ advertises beta availability
in Argentina, Mexico and Chile; Colombia, Peru, Uruguay and Brazil are forthcoming.
Other-country priority registration is not operating matching availability.
Its web-version FAQ describes Android as the full experience, iOS as forthcoming
and the website as informational. Download links inspected on that page point
back to `/#`, not an established working store path. Free base networking and
optional premium are advertised; exact premium cost and account access remain
unverified. No member-count, successful-match or identity-verification claim was
independently confirmed.

The [terms](https://cofound.lat/terminos/), dated May 15, name Nex Solutions Labs,
require adults and prohibit spam, unauthorized advertising and unauthorized access
to other users' data. Their user-content license is for display within the
service, not an import/reuse grant to this app. Verification is described as
optional and indicative, unlike the homepage's stronger verification wording.
The document explicitly calls itself a starting point requiring production legal
review; no compliance conclusion is drawn here.

The [privacy notice](https://cofound.lat/privacidad/), also dated May 15, describes
account/profile/usage/technical data and optional identity documents, with
authenticated-member visibility and providers. Its deletion/retention and security
statements are declarations, not tested implementation. The separate
[child-protection page](https://cofound.lat/proteccion-menores/) likewise states
adult-only access; its moderation measures have not been audited.

### App-store counterevidence

The previously held [Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nex.cofound)
describes LATAM matching but identifies developer NEXium / Overall Consulting
S.A.C. Its developer website link returned 404 and is not the cofound.lat domain.
The listing's no-data-collected declaration conflicts with the linked policy's
account/profile/device/usage collection description. An age content rating is
not proof of membership eligibility. These observations require reconciliation,
not an assumption that every same-named product shares one operator.

That [linked developer privacy notice](https://nex-solutions-labs.github.io/cofound-files/privacy-policy.html),
dated May 9, describes professional profiles, messages, usage/device data and
adult-only access. The shared naming/domain clues are insufficient to prove the
exact website/app/legal-operator relationship. Do not merge brands, install the
app, upload identity documents or present its privacy declarations as audited
without further review.

## CoFound at cofound.space

The [public Spanish page](https://cofound.space/) explicitly presents a forthcoming
service and waitlist. Its FAQ says launch starts in Spain, with later expansion
to other Spanish-speaking markets; a worldwide waitlist is not worldwide access.
Free and EUR 4.99/month premium tiers, swipe/chat features and profile verification
are advertised future offers, not tested operating capabilities. Legal/footer
links inspected in the reader resolve to the same landing page rather than
substantive policy documents. Keep this as a pre-launch lead; do not count it as
an operating matcher, use its displayed sample conversations as real outcomes,
or merge its branding with cofound.lat/cofound.biz. No waitlist or form submitted.

## Method, limits and next evidence

Queries covered Spanish-language co-founder discovery and a targeted recheck of
the held cofound.lat lead. Search snippets were leads only. Actual first-party
homepages, linked policies and the developer-authored app listing were read.
The in-app browser failed to initialize; a separate isolated Chrome context
rendered cofound.lat and expanded only its public country/web-version FAQs.
The owned research tab was closed afterward. No account, private page, individual
profile route, external checkout, support email or submission was used.

Next review should establish WeFoundy's substantive policies and access limits,
resolve cofound.lat's real download/operator/privacy inconsistencies, and revisit
cofound.space only after operating access exists. Native-language member/device
acceptance, geographic eligibility, fees and exact reuse/contact permissions
remain separate gates. This targeted review is not exhaustive country coverage.

Market-research guidance required direct reads, counterevidence and separate
discovery/access/permission decisions; no marketing statement became a verified
integration or catalogue admission.
