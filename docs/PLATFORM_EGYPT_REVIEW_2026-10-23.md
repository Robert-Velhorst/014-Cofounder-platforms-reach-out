# Egypt discovery review — October 23, 2026

## Decision

Engage Egypt is a candidate dedicated co-founder discovery route; Startup Egypt
is a distinct, indirect community lead. Neither has been added to the runtime
catalogue in this research-only increment. No workflow admission, account,
integration, price guarantee or contact permission has been established. The
worldwide requirement remains incomplete, not satisfied by a finite list.

## First-party observations

### Engage Egypt / إنجيج إيجيبت

The [English co-founder guide](https://egyptengage.com/find-cofounder-egypt)
describes founder-posted projects with co-founder roles, applicant questions and
founder selection. Pitches are not separately moderated; membership applications
are reviewed. Public pitch pages preview roles; applications normally require
membership unless the founder enables external applications. Posting and applying
are advertised free. These are provider descriptions, not tested entitlement or
successful introductions.

The [Arabic guide](https://egyptengage.com/ar/find-cofounder-egypt) describes the
same model. It supports Arabic-language discoverability, not acceptance of every
Arabic/RTL account or application workflow. The
[membership page](https://egyptengage.com/apply) describes administrator-reviewed
applications; no application was opened beyond its public text or submitted.

The [homepage](https://egyptengage.com/) advertises public reading/search and
agent use. This is not authority for this app to act as a member, harvest profiles,
reuse personal data or send messages. Its linked
[agents page](https://egyptengage.com/agents) yielded only a title in the reader;
attempts to read `/terms` and `/privacy` were unsuccessful. This establishes a
retrieval limit, not absence of policies. Operator identity, substantive policies,
age/geographic eligibility, member access and any permitted reuse/contact path
remain to be checked before a catalogue/action decision.

### Startup Egypt

The [current homepage](https://startupegypt.org.eg/) advertises free enthusiast
membership, paid registered-startup membership and networking events. It labels
its broader matchmaking engine as forthcoming and full-membership-only. Do not
present that engine as operating dedicated co-founder matching.

The [membership terms](https://startupegypt.org.eg/terms), dated July 19, 2026,
describe Egyptian registered-company eligibility, identity/company documents,
application review, invoicing and non-refundable annual dues after payment.
Those terms do not explain the homepage's separate free enthusiast route; its
actual eligibility and terms remain unresolved. No fee amount was verified.
The terms prohibit unauthorized commercial solicitation and do not guarantee
programme admission or funding. These observations are not a legal compliance
assessment or permission to import/contact members.

The similarly named `startup-egypt.org` returned HTTP 502 when read. No common
operator or equivalent membership is established; keep the domains distinct.

## Held counterevidence and method

- Foundersbase's Egypt country page is an existing brand variant, not evidence
  of an independent matching pool or verified Egyptian member entitlement.
- Egypt Innovate appeared in government-source search results, but the actual
  ITIDA/TIEC press-release read failed with HTTP 502. No operating-feature claim
  or catalogue addition is based on that search excerpt.
- No individual profile, private group, external form, account, purchase or
  message was collected or submitted. Public listing visibility is not consent.

Queries: `Egypt cofounder matching startup community official` and
`مصر ابحث شريك مؤسس منصة رواد اعمال`, followed by the direct reads linked above.
Search results were leads, not evidence of complete national coverage. Native
Arabic member/device acceptance, other Egyptian communities, current accounts,
pricing and policy review remain open. This report changes research evidence
only; it has no application behavior or runtime qualification claim.

Market-research guidance influenced this review by requiring first-party reads,
counterevidence, explicit unresolved conditions and a held decision instead of
treating marketing or search snippets as verified integrations.
