# Co-founder discovery and assisted outreach

This context helps founders discover people and privately record their own reviewed outreach and progress.

## Language

**Recorded partnership outcome**:
An account owner's dated report about progress with a candidate, not independently verified agreement, another person's consent or a legal partnership.
_Avoid_: Verified partnership, automatic success

**Candidate assessment**:
A recorded evaluation of a candidate's fit with one founder's venture preferences, not an endorsement or permission to contact that person.
_Avoid_: Contact permission, guaranteed match

**Email token redemption evidence**:
A record that an account's one-use email token was consumed for its then-current email address, not independent verification of the person's identity or proof about earlier verified flags.
_Avoid_: Verified identity, legacy-account classification

**Reviewed re-verification requirement**:
An operator-approved requirement for one account to demonstrate control of its email address again, without deleting its workspace or asserting that its earlier verification was illegitimate.
_Avoid_: Legacy purge, identity reset, deletion suspension
